This page sets out the terms that apply when you use kesintimivar.com. By browsing the
site you accept them.

## What the service is

kesintimivar.com is a free information service that compiles electricity outage
announcements for Northern Cyprus from public sources and shows them on a single
page.

It is free, requires no account, and has no paid version.

## What we do not promise

This is the most important section; please read it.

**The information is not a guarantee.** Announced work can be cancelled, finish
earlier than planned, or run long. Every time and duration on the site is an
**estimate**, and is labelled as one on the card.

**It may be incomplete.** We can only compile outages that were announced. An
unannounced fault may never appear here. If a source site goes down or changes
its format, information may be delayed.

**It may contain errors.** Announcement texts are parsed automatically. That
process can produce a wrong time, a missing village name, or a wrong
classification. We correct such records — but they have to be noticed first.

**This is not KIB-TEK's official site** and has no connection to the utility.
The definitive answer is always their own announcement.

## Do not rely on it alone for critical decisions

Do not use this site as the **only** basis for a decision whose consequences
could be serious.

If someone in your home depends on a mains-powered medical device, if your
business has a cold chain, or if an outage would have serious consequences for
you, confirm directly with KIB-TEK and make your own contingency plan. What that
can look like is covered in our
[long outage guide](/en/guides/long-outage).

The guides give general information. They are not a substitute for medical,
legal or professional advice. Work on an electrical installation is a job for a
qualified electrician.

## Using the content

**Outage records** are factual information compiled from public announcements:
date, time, place name, and kind of outage. You may use them; we ask that you
credit this site and link to the original announcement.

**The guide articles** are ours. For short quotations, a credit is enough; to
republish one in full elsewhere, please write to us first.

**The text of the source announcements** belongs to the utility and the news
outlets. That is why we do not store or republish their writing: we extract only
structured facts and link back to the original.

**Automated access.** If you want to read the site by machine, do it at a
reasonable rate and identify yourself — the same way we do with our own sources.
We may block access heavy enough to degrade the service. If you need the data
regularly, write to us and let us talk about it.
